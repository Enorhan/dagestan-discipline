Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-09

This policy explains how MatFlow handles account data, training activity, subscription state, and support requests.

What we collect

  • Account information such as email address, display name, username, and sport preferences.
  • Training data such as sessions, activity logs, learning progress, streak data, and saved program state.
  • Subscription and billing metadata required to verify premium access.
  • Product analytics and buffered error reports used to diagnose failures and improve product quality.

How we use data

  • To authenticate you and sync your training data across sessions.
  • To generate and maintain personalized programs and premium access state.
  • To troubleshoot crashes, checkout failures, and account problems.
  • To analyze onboarding, workout, and billing flows so the app can be improved.

Third-party services

  • Supabase is used for authentication and application data storage.
  • Apple and Stripe may be used for subscription checkout, billing management, entitlement verification, and payment webhooks depending on platform.
  • Vercel Analytics is used for product event tracking.

Retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings › Account › Delete account. Deletion is immediate, irreversible, and removes your profile, training sessions, gameplans, uploaded media, social posts, and subscription metadata. Outside that flow, we retain data only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the app, provide support, and maintain billing records required by law.

You can also export a copy of your data from Settings › Account › Export my data. The export is generated on demand and downloaded as a JSON archive containing your profile, training sessions, gameplans, library entries, and social activity.

Your rights and choices

  • You can manage or cancel your subscription from Settings › Manage subscription, which opens the App Store (iOS) or your billing portal (web).
  • You can restore previously purchased subscriptions from Settings › Restore purchases.
  • You can permanently delete your account in-app from Settings › Delete account.
  • You can request a portable copy of your data from Settings › Export my data.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, and the CCPA, including the right to access, correct, port, restrict, or delete your personal data, and the right to object to processing. Contact support to exercise these rights.
  • You can stop using the app at any time.

App Tracking Transparency

MatFlow does not track you across other companies' apps and websites. We do not use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) for tracking, advertising, or sharing with data brokers. Product analytics are first-party and tied to your in-app account so we can diagnose failures.

Contact

For privacy or account requests, contact support@matflow.app.
